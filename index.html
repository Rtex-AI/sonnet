SONNET KM

One-stop garment manufacturing portal — wireframes

The complete system, A to Z, in one portal: styles, costing, orders, purchasing, cutting, sewing, labels, receiving, warehouse, shipping, notes, finance (A/P · A/R · journal), reports and administration. Everything the legacy EICON apps, GIMS layer and Accpac ERP did — self-contained, nothing external. All names and figures are illustrative samples.
